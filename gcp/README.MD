## ⚠️ Deprecation Notice

Effective on **March 1st 2025** we are **removing the capability to onboard Cloud Accounts with Terraform for GCP**. Instead, **Google Cloud Shell** will be the preferred method for onboarding new AWS Cloud Accounts on **Omnistrate**, both for Service Provider Hosted offerings and Bring Your Own Account.  

### Why This Change?  
This update ensures the **best experience during onboarding**.  

### Impact  
- **Existing onboarded accounts will not be affected.**  
- The **existing Terraform stack will no longer be updated** to support new features.  

For more details, please refer to our [documentation](https://docs.omnistrate.com/getting-started/getting-started/) or contact support.
